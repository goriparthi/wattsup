Wattsup with your charger?

Live watts, volts and amps in your menu bar, and a charger check that tells you whether the brick is the bottleneck.

Download for Mac View on GitHub

macOS 14 or later. Apple silicon MacBooks. MIT licensed.

Pick your numbers.

Choose what lives in the menu bar: watts, volts, amps, battery percent, time to full or empty, temperature, or charger load. Plugged in, the numbers are the charger input. On battery, they are the battery pack.

Click for a live flow from charger to Mac to battery, with watts on every leg.

Charger check

Is your charger holding you back?

Hotel desk, airport gate, a borrowed brick. Wattsup compares what your Mac is asking for, system load plus what the battery wants to charge, against what the charger can deliver.

  • Flags a brick pegged at its limit.
  • Flags a battery that drains while plugged in.
  • Suggests a wattage that would remove the bottleneck.
  • Remembers every charger you have used, with peak load for each.

Privacy

It only listens to your Mac.

Wattsup reads Apple's own battery and power telemetry on your machine. Nothing leaves it, and the source is right there to check.

No networkIt never phones home.
No accountNothing to sign up for.
No adminNo password prompts or helpers.
No trackingNo analytics of any kind.

Install

Two minutes, start to finish.

  1. Download the latest DMG from the releases page.
  2. Drag Wattsup into Applications and open it.
  3. Pick your menu bar numbers.
Download the DMG Source on GitHub
