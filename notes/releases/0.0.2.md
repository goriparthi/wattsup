A big step up from 0.0.1: a desktop widget, a charger report card, thermals, notifications and history that survives restarts.

- Charger report card: voltage under load, measured cable resistance (which separates a weak cable from a weak charger), whether it delivers its rating, drop-outs and heat. Each check waits until it has real data.
- Clearer charger advice: a negotiation phase right after plugging in is never judged, and a 3 A capped charger now says check the cable instead of buy a bigger charger.
- Thermals: fan speeds and chip temperatures next to the power that drives them, plus a ranked list of the apps using the most energy.
- Notifications for a charger that can't keep up, a cable limiting power, a hot battery, a chosen charge level, and unplugging at low battery. Each can be turned off.
- History is kept on disk for 30 days, with 24 hour and 7 day charts, daily energy in watt hours with optional cost, and a battery health trend with a projection to 80%.
- Explains why a plugged in Mac is not charging, such as a hold near 80% for Optimized Charging or a charge limit.
- Desktop widget, AirPods and Bluetooth accessory batteries, Low Power Mode shown in the menu bar, a first run welcome, a warning when macOS hides the readout, and spoken descriptions for VoiceOver.
- Dashboard: larger type and higher contrast, battery cells drawn as compartments of a pack, and fans that spin with their speed and read Off when stopped.
- Fixes: no more -0.0 W, the time reading never disappears near full, and a cleaner, quieter look throughout.
