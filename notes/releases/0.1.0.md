First release of Wattsup.

- Menu bar readout of watts, volts and amps, plus any of Mac power, battery power, wall draw, charger load, percent, time, temperature and health. Pick and order them in Settings, on one line or stacked in two rows, colored by meaning inside an outlined capsule.
- Popover with a live flow from charger to Mac to battery and a five minute chart.
- Dashboard with power, voltage, current, charge and temperature history, every USB-C Power Delivery profile the charger offers, conversion loss, battery health, cycles and per cell voltages.
- Charger check: tells you when the charger is the bottleneck or can't keep up, suggests a size that would fix it, and remembers every charger you use.
- Daily update check against GitHub releases with verified in-place install.
