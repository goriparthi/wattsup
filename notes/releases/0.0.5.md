A charger library: rank the chargers you own and give them names.

- Every charger you have used is ranked, by Overall, Power, Efficiency, Quality or Recently used. Overall weighs the power it can deliver, its efficiency and its report card, and leaves out anything not measured yet.
- Name a charger with the pencil, such as Hotel Anker, and the name shows in the popover and on the Charger card. Names stay on your Mac and are never written to the log.
