Test your charger and cable on the spot, get a receipt for every charge, and find out which charger to pack.

- Charger and cable test: click Test now on the Charger card. Wattsup briefly loads the Mac for about 20 seconds and grades voltage sag, cable resistance and the charger's rating right away. It stops if you unplug or the chip gets hot.
- Charging receipts: each charge gets a receipt with the charger, start and end percent, watt hours added, average and peak watts, time to 80%, and how long the charger held it back. An optional notification sends it when you unplug.
- Which charger to pack: the charger library now names the smallest charger you own that keeps up with how you actually use your Mac, and the fastest one.
- Live graph in the menu bar: a new reading draws the last 60 seconds of power.
- Battery care: hours spent full on the charger, hours hot, and deep discharges, from your own history, with plain advice.
- Fixed: while fast charging, Mac uses could read far too high. It now always balances with the charger and battery.
