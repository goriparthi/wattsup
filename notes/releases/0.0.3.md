Battery capacity in watt hours, a lifetime record from the battery itself, and a tidier dashboard.

- Battery capacity: how much energy the battery holds today against its design (about 96 of 99 Wh on a 16 inch MacBook Pro), how much is stored now, and how long that lasts at the current draw.
- Lifetime, as the battery recorded it: its highest draw and charge current, temperature range and operating time.
- History now sits below the Charger, Battery and Thermals cards, so the dashboard reads top to bottom.
