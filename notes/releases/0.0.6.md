Better charger memory: tell identical chargers apart, combine cables, and see exactly when you used each one.

- Apple chargers are now recognized by their serial number, so two identical bricks stay separate. A charger you had already used keeps its history and name.
- Same charger as: in the charger library, use the ⋯ menu to combine entries for one physical brick on different cables. It ranks on its best cable, with each cable's numbers one click away, and Separate undoes it.
- Last seen now shows the exact time, such as Yesterday 4:32 PM, with the full date and first use on hover.
