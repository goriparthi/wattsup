Charging demystified: a new look and a few fixes.

- New app icon: a purple charger wired to a red battery, in Finder, the Dock and the app switcher.
- While the dashboard or Settings is open, Wattsup now behaves like a normal app: it shows in Cmd+Tab and the Dock with a proper menu bar, then returns to the menu bar alone once you close the window.
- History charts no longer draw a smooth line across time Wattsup was not running; gaps stay empty.
