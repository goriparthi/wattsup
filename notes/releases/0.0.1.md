First public build of Wattsup: live power for your MacBook, in the menu bar.

- Menu bar readout of up to 14 readings, picked and ordered in Settings, on one line or two rows, colored by meaning inside an outlined capsule. Hover any number to see what it is.
- Popover with a battery tank that fills and drains, and a hybrid car style power flow from charger to Mac to battery, including powered accessories such as an iPhone.
- Charger check: says when the charger or its cable is the bottleneck, suggests a size that would fix it, and grades charger efficiency.
- Dashboard with history charts, every USB-C Power Delivery profile, battery health, cycles, cell voltages, energy mode and every charger you have used.
- Energy mode switch, daily update check that can be turned off, and local logs with crash capture for troubleshooting.
